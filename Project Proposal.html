Project Overview

This document presents our group's proposed approach for the IT3081 Statistical Modelling Group Assignment. Our project addresses a real-world industry challenge posed by BladeGen Tech (Pvt) Ltd, a technology partner serving a large Food & Beverage (F&B) chain operating across 80+ outlets in Sri Lanka.

The core challenge is: given transaction-level point-of-sale (POS) data, can we build a statistical model that predicts what category of product a customer is most likely to purchase? This enables the business to deliver targeted promotions, optimise inventory, and personalise the in-store customer experience.

Bonus Opportunity Applied: This project qualifies for Bonus Opportunity 1 (+10%) as it is a genuine industry consultancy engagement, with an official project approval letter from BladeGen Tech dated 20 September 2026.
2

Problem Statement

The F&B chain processes thousands of point-of-sale transactions daily across its outlet network. Despite holding rich transactional data — including customer demographics, loyalty programme membership, time of purchase, payment method, and product categories — the business currently has no predictive capability to anticipate customer purchasing behaviour.

Our group will act as a statistical modelling consultancy team to analyse this data and develop a predictive model capable of answering the following business question:

Research Question:
Given contextual information about a customer and their visit (e.g. time of day, day of week, outlet, customer type, payment method, loyalty membership), which product category — Beverage, Food, Merchandising, or Deals — is the customer most likely to purchase?

Proposed Methodology

We will address all 12 required consultancy tasks as defined in the assignment brief. The table below outlines each task, our approach, and the type of deliverable.

# Task Our Approach Deliverable
1 Problem Statement & Industry Context Define the F&B chain's challenge: lack of predictive purchase intelligence across 80+ outlets. Frame using real BladeGen Tech data. Report
2 Literature Review Review 15+ recent research papers covering: machine learning for purchase prediction, F&B demand forecasting, logistic regression, time series, PCA, Bayesian methods, experimental design. Report
3 Dataset Understanding & Descriptive Analysis Load, clean and profile the dataset using R. Generate visualisations (sales by outlet, time-of-day, category, customer type, payment method). Produce summary statistics. R Script
4 Statistical Inference Conduct hypothesis tests: T-test (local vs foreign spend), ANOVA (revenue by outlet), Chi-Square (payment method vs customer type), Kruskal-Wallis (qty by time of day). Report p-values at α = 0.05. R Script
5 Predictive Statistical Modelling Train a Multinomial Logistic Regression model (using nnet package) to predict MainCategory. Use an 80/20 train-test split. Evaluate using confusion matrix, accuracy, F1-score. Compare with a Random Forest baseline. R Script
6 Experimental Design Discuss applicability of CRD and RCBD in this context. Propose: outlets as blocking units, time-of-day as treatment. Relate to A/B testing of discount promotions. Report
7 Principal Component Analysis (PCA) One-hot encode categorical variables, apply prcomp(), inspect scree plot, and assess dimensionality reduction potential. Generate PCA biplot. R Script
8 Bayesian Methods Train a Naïve Bayes classifier (e1071 package) for MainCategory. Discuss prior probability selection and compare performance against logistic regression. R Script
9 Time Series Analysis Aggregate daily transaction counts from OrderDate. Plot ACF/PACF, fit an ARIMA model using auto.arima(), forecast next 30 days of transaction volume. R Script
10 Innovation Proposal Propose a Customer Intelligence & Dynamic Offer Framework: a real-time scoring engine integrated at the POS terminal that predicts likely purchase category and triggers personalised promotions via the loyalty programme. Report
11 Expert Interview Interview a Data Analyst / BI professional from the F&B or retail sector to validate our approach and gather industry insights on demand prediction challenges. Interview
12 Recommendations Provide 5–7 data-driven strategic and operational recommendations for the F&B chain based on our statistical findings (e.g. peak time targeting, loyalty programme enhancement, product pairing strategies). Report
